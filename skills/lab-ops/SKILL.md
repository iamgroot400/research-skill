# Lab operations

**You prepare; the human submits.** Never submit forms, register, pay, accept terms or send email on the user's behalf. Draft everything and hand it over. Never guess personal data: leave clearly marked blanks like `[[PASSPORT NO.]]`.

## Deadlines

- Keep `lab/deadlines.md` sorted by date, always with the timezone (many CS venues use AoE, UTC-12).
- Check every date on the official CFP page when web access is available, and store the link. Otherwise mark it **unverified**. Dates move, so recheck within 2 weeks of any deadline.
- Work backwards to internal milestones: draft complete, `qa-check` pass, co-author review, and buffer.

## Submission checklist

Build it from the venue's actual call for papers and author kit, not from memory. Cover:
- page limits (main / references / appendix), template and version, font and margin rules
- anonymization: self-citations, acknowledgments, funding lines, repo links, **PDF metadata**, file names
- supplementary material and code/data policy, reproducibility or ethics checklist, LLM-use disclosure policy
- conflicts of interest, and author profiles (e.g. OpenReview) for **all** co-authors

Check the current draft against it and report pass / fail / unknown for each item.

## Travel & registration

Produce a timeline covering early-bird registration, visa invitation letters (lead time can be months), travel grants and student-volunteer deadlines, poster size and format, and talk length. Draft the emails and letters.

## Paperwork

Fill drafts from what is in `lab/` and what the user gives you. List every remaining blank at the end, and who has to supply it.

## After

Append a notebook entry. Report any deadline within 14 days at the top of your reply.
